About Us

The D' Chimney is the first chain to offer a cosy and welcoming atmosphere based out of Greater Lat Hulasganj, Jehanabad, Bihar, creating a perfect setting for relaxation and socialising. The interior is tastefully decorated with a blend of modern and rustic elements, providing a comfortable space for guests to unwind. It's an ideal place to meet friends, celebrate special occasions, or simply enjoy a fun-filled evening. Whether you're a regular or a first-time visitor, you can expect friendly and professional assistance throughout your dining experience. Overall, a relaxed and inviting environment, a diverse menu, and a commitment to excellent service It's a place where customers can unwind, enjoy great food, and create lasting memories.
